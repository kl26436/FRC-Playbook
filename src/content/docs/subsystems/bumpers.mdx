Bumpers are the part of the robot that hits things, and the part inspectors measure first. They also come off and go back on at every event, usually in a hurry.

## What we decided

| Choice | What we picked |
| --- | --- |
| Layout | Two halves, front and rear, meeting at the middle of each side |
| Backing | 3/4 in plywood, 5 in tall, from 2 in to 7 in above the carpet |
| Padding | Two 2.5 in pool noodles, stacked |
| Cover | Cordura fabric, one red set and one blue set |
| Corners | An aluminium angle brace inside each corner |
| Mounting | Angle brackets on the boards, bolted down into 1/4-20 rivnuts in the tops of the frame rails |
| Fitting | Drop each half on from above. Four bolts a half |

## Why

- **Two halves.** Each half is a U that drops over one end of the robot. Nothing has to slide past the mechanisms.
- **Bolts from above.** They go down into the rail tops, so nobody has to reach under the robot. The drawing's target is two people, under five minutes.
- **Brackets only where the rail is free.** The modules, the mast braces, the breaker and the climber already use parts of the rail tops. The brackets take what is left.

### What changed along the way

- **Pockets for bolt heads.** The frame's own bolts have heads on the outside of the rails. The boards are pocketed 5/8 in wide and 1/4 in deep over each one so they sit flat.
- **A bracket moved.** The rear side brackets moved 0.8 in forward, because their rivnut landed on the cross member's bolts inside the rail.

## Checked against the rules

The bumper drawing lists these checks. Read the current game manual for the exact wording before building.

| Rule on the drawing | Limit | This robot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R403, bumper from the frame | 4 in at most | 3.25 in |
| R404, hard parts out from the frame | 1.25 in at most | 0.75 in |
| R404, padding beyond the hard parts | 2 in at least | 2.5 in |

With bumpers the robot is 34.5 in square. The bumpers weigh 14.3 lb as modelled.

## Not proven yet

- **The fitting time** is a target. Nobody has timed it.
- **Rivnut hole size.** The drawing gives 0.391 in for a 1/4-20 rivnut and says to check your rivnut first.

## Your turn

For a new game:

- [ ] Read the bumper rules first. They change, and they are inspected with a tape measure.
- [ ] Decide how the bumpers come off before designing anything that sits on the frame rails.
- [ ] Mark where the rail tops are already taken, and put the brackets in what is left.
- [ ] Time a bumper change with two people. Then do it again with two different people.
