Each mechanism has its own page of steps. This page is the order they go onto the robot.

The order is not free. Some parts are riveted to parts of a different mechanism. Some sit underneath what comes next. Get it wrong and something has to come off again.

## Rules that apply everywhere

| Joint | Fastener | Drill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rivet | 5/32 in aluminium blind rivet. Pick the grip length to suit the thickness being joined | 0.161 in (#20), drilled through the mating part |
| #10 screw passing through | #10-32 socket head or button head | 0.196 in (#9) |
| #10 screw into thin tube or sheet | #10-32 rivnut | 0.297 in. Check against the rivnuts you buy |
| #8 screw into sheet | #8-32 rivnut | 0.266 in |
| 1/4 in screw into a rail | 1/4-20 rivnut | 0.391 in |
| Screw into a printed part | #10-32 heat-set insert, pressed in with a soldering iron | 0.262 in |

Which rivet to use depends on the total thickness it has to grip:

| Thickness being joined | Rivet |
| --- | --- |
| 0.126 to 0.187 in | 97517A032 |
| 0.188 to 0.25 in | 97517A035 |
| 0.251 to 0.375 in | 97517A040 |
| 0.376 to 0.5 in | 97517A041 |

- **Lock nuts.** Where a step says "and lock nut", the screw goes right through and takes a nylon-insert nut.
- **Thread locker.** Use it on screws into tapped aluminium and into motor faces. Do not use it on screws into printed parts or heat-set inserts.
- **Set CAN IDs first.** Give every motor controller and sensor its ID before it goes on the robot.

## Tools to have out

- Rivet gun and rivnut tool
- Drills: #20 and #9, plus 0.266, 0.297 and 0.391 in for rivnuts
- Hex keys: 1/16, 3/32, 1/8 and 5/32 in
- Soldering iron with insert tips
- A long-reach nosepiece for the rivet gun, for the mast brace
